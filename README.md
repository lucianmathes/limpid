# LIMPID -- Layer-wise Investigation of Measurements on Positron Implantation and Diffusion

LIMPID helps you with analyzing your positron annihilation depth profiles. It will fit the solution of the diffusion equation to your measurement data and thereby determine the positron diffusion length in your sample.
